About Dr. Harshit Gupta

Dr. Harshit Gupta, BAMS, MD Ayurveda (Ayurvedic Physiology), is an Ayurvedic physician with three years of Junior Resident experience and clinical, academic and research exposure at the All India Institute of Ayurveda, New Delhi.

His interests include Ayurvedic physiology, personalized healthcare, preventive health, lifestyle assessment, nutrition and physical wellbeing.
